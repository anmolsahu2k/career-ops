# CMU Resources for International Students — Distilled

Source: https://www.cmu.edu/career/students-and-alumni/international-students/index.html
Captured: 2026-04-25

## Tier 1 — Use these immediately

### GoinGlobal (via Handshake)
- **Access**: Log into [cmu.joinhandshake.com](https://cmu.joinhandshake.com/) → search "GoinGlobal" → article at https://cmu.joinhandshake.com/articles/1075
- **Why critical**: Only tool with **H1B sponsorship-history filtering**
- **Sub-tools**:
  - **H1B Plus database** — companies by sponsorship petition history (filter by industry, role, year)
  - **Top OPT Employers** — companies that actively hire OPT/CPT
  - **City / country career guides** — useful for India backup
- **How to use for current applications**:
  1. Run Sierra, Lindy, Cohere through H1B Plus before applying — confirms they have sponsorship history
  2. Run any new target company through it FIRST
  3. Sort H1B Plus by your industry → discover new sponsor-friendly companies you haven't considered

### CMU Alumni Online Community
- **URL**: [community.cmu.edu/s/](https://community.cmu.edu/s/)
- **Why critical**: Higher response rate than LinkedIn cold outreach (alumni opted in here specifically for student outreach)
- **Use case**:
  1. Search target company name → message any alumni
  2. Use the Track A2 template (15 sentences, mention CMU + your project + ask for referral or 15-min call)
  3. Industry Insights events also linked here — attend any in your target field

### Handshake
- **URL**: [cmu.joinhandshake.com](https://cmu.joinhandshake.com/)
- **Set up**:
  1. Saved search: Internship + Posted last 7 days + "Sponsorship friendly" filter
  2. Daily email alerts on
  3. RSVP to any career fair in your target field

## Tier 2 — Worth knowing about

### CMU International Alumni Networks
- **URL**: https://www.cmu.edu/engage/alumni/get-involved/networks/international/index.html
- **When useful**: India backup track — Indian CMU alumni network for warm intros at Indian tech companies (Razorpay, CRED, Microsoft IDC, etc.)

### CPDC SCS/Tech career consultant
- **Book via**: main CPDC site, ask specifically for the SCS or Tech-track consultant
- **Best use**: 1:1 review of cover-letter blurbs and Block H answers BEFORE you submit Sierra/Lindy. Drop-in hours typically 1-4pm at the CUC, lower level.

### Student Academic Success Center
- **URL**: https://www.cmu.edu/student-success/
- **Best use**: One-time language polish on the OIE email or any cover letter, if you want a free second pair of eyes (limited usefulness for this timeline)

## Important caveat

> "Carnegie Mellon is NOT an eligible employer for the purpose of STEM OPT Extension."

**Implication**: A summer faculty RA at CMU won't count toward STEM OPT extension eligibility post-graduation. Doesn't affect your summer 2026 plans, but if you're choosing between a CMU RA and an industry intern at the same comp, the industry one builds STEM OPT eligibility too.

## Concrete pre-application checklist (per role)

Before submitting any application, run through:

- [ ] Verify company has sponsorship history via GoinGlobal H1B Plus
- [ ] Search CMU Alumni Online Community for current employees → send Track A2 message
- [ ] Search LinkedIn for "Carnegie Mellon" + company name → send Track A2 message to any match
- [ ] Verify the careers URL is live and the req still says "open"
- [ ] Confirm role accepts CPT (some F-1-friendly companies still won't take CPT — verify in JD or via alumni)
- [ ] Submit with the appropriate PDF (sierra/lindy/cohere PDFs from `career-ops/output/`)
