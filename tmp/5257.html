Become an ABI Home Care and Community Life Advocate

Are you looking for a job that actually matters? A team that treats you with respect? A purpose you can be proud of every day?

ABI Resources is hiring.
We help people with brain injuries and disabilities live better lives, right in their homes. You don’t need a fancy degree. If you’re kind, dependable, and ready to help you’re already what we’re looking for.

What You’ll Do

  • Help real people live safe, independent lives at home

  • Be a friend, a support, and a positive presence

  • Help with daily tasks like meals, rides, cleaning, or just being there

  • Work in your local area

  • Be respected for who you are and the care you give

What You’ll Get

  • Reliable, steady scheduling (weekday and weekend shifts available)

  • A real team that listens and values YOU

  • Training and support to help you grow

  • The chance to proudly call yourself an ABI Life Advocate – someone who changes lives every day

What You Need to Bring

  • Be 18 or older

  • A car, a license, and proof of insurance

  • A kind heart and a strong sense of responsibility

  • A background check and reference

No experience? That’s okay. We’ll train you.

Roles and Responsibilities

  • Comprehensive Support: Help clients with daily living and independence

  • Professional Friendship: Build strong, healthy, respectful relationships

  • Daily Living Assistance: Support personal care and household needs

  • Health and Wellness: Join them at medical visits, encourage healthy habits, and support social connection

To Join ABI Resources

  • High school diploma or equivalent

  • 1 year of disability experience is great (but not required)

  • Valid driver’s license and insurance

  • Background check and personal or professional reference

  • Must pass a drug screening

  • Must be ready to make a real difference

Where We Work

We serve people in over 150 towns across Connecticut—from Bridgeport to Hartford, Waterbury to New London, Stamford to Manchester, and everywhere in between. If you live in Connecticut, we likely have clients near you.

Equal Opportunity

ABI Resources is proud to hire people of all races, ages, religions, genders, abilities, and backgrounds. We believe everyone deserves to be treated with dignity—and that includes our team.
