ABI Resources is hiring. We help people with brain injuries and disabilities live safer and more independent lives at home across Connecticut. You do not need a fancy degree. If you are kind, dependable, and ready to serve, you are exactly who we want on our team.

What you will do

  • Help people live safely and independently at home

  • Be a steady support and a positive presence

  • Assist with daily tasks such as meals, rides, light cleaning, and companionship

  • Join clients at appointments and community activities

  • Document services with clarity and accuracy using simple tools

  • Work in your local area

You will see real progress in daily routines. You will hear sincere gratitude in the voices of the people you support. You will feel the difference your consistency makes.

What you will get

  • Reliable, steady scheduling with weekday and weekend options

  • A real team that listens and values your voice

  • Paid training and ongoing support to help you grow

  • The chance to proudly call yourself an ABI Life Advocate

What you need to bring

  • Age 18 or older

  • Your own car, a valid driver license, and proof of insurance

  • A strong sense of responsibility and a kind heart

  • Ability to plan, prioritize, and keep excellent records

  • Clear communication and relationship skills that build trust

  • Background check and reference

  • Drug screening

No experience required. We provide training and mentor support.

Roles and responsibilities

  • Comprehensive support
    Help clients with daily living, safety, and independence

  • Professional friendship
    Build healthy, respectful relationships with strong boundaries

  • Daily living assistance
    Support personal care and household needs with dignity

  • Health and wellness
    Join clients at medical visits, encourage healthy habits, and support social connection

  • Organization and documentation
    Plan the day, follow the care plan, and record visits with accuracy

Who thrives here

  • People who keep their word and show up on time

  • People who can organize a day, follow a plan, and adapt in the moment

  • People who listen deeply, speak clearly, and build trust with families and teams

  • People who take pride in consistent action and results

Important fit check

This role requires a dependable personal vehicle every day. If you do not have a car, a current license, and insurance, this role is not a match. If you prefer to coast, arrive late, or avoid responsibility, this will not be a fit. We honor teammates who take ownership and deliver for the people we serve.

To join ABI Resources

  • High school diploma or equivalent

  • One year of disability support experience is helpful but not required

  • Valid driver license and insurance

  • Background check and personal or professional reference

  • Drug screening

  • A steady commitment to make a real difference

Where we work

We serve people in more than one hundred fifty towns across Connecticut, from Bridgeport to Hartford, Waterbury to New London, Stamford to Manchester, and many communities in between. If you live in Connecticut, we likely have clients near you.

Equal opportunity

ABI Resources welcomes team members of every race, age, religion, gender, ability, and background. We believe every person deserves dignity and respect, including our team and the families we serve.
