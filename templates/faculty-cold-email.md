### For healthcare ML / clinical-ML labs (Padman, Dubrawski, Lipton, Perer, Heidari, Rosenfeld, Tayur, Scheller-Wolf, Ravikumar)

- Body paragraph: tie their human-side question to your technical work
- Resume: MLE if research-flavored, SDE if engineering-flavored

### For Heinz / Tepper business-tech labs (Padman, Beibei Li, Tayur, Singh, Srinivasan)

- Resume: MLE for ML-leaning labs, SDE for systems-leaning labs

### For systems / security / CyLab labs (Sekar, Bauer, Parno, Miller, Faloutsos)

- Subject hook: lead with Byju's microservices / cloud migration / DRM
- Lead paragraph: 100% test coverage on Scheduling microservice, AWS to GCP migration ($400K savings), 10+ vertical refactor

- Resume: **SDE Anmol's Resume(28-02-26)_v3-FINAL.pdf**

## Sending checklist (per email)

- [ ] Verify the prof's email address from their CMU page (don't trust old listings)
- [ ] Read at least one specific paper of theirs from 2025-2026; cite it correctly (title + venue + year)
- [ ] Make sure the body paragraph is genuinely substantive, not a generic compliment
- [ ] Attach the right resume (SDE vs MLE) per the archetype guide above
- [ ] Send Saturday morning (preferred) or Sunday morning. Avoid Friday afternoon.
- [ ] Spot-check the prof's "News" section before sending; if last activity is >6 months stale, deprioritize

## One-bump follow-up (Wednesday after no-reply)

Send a single short follow-up to anyone who hasn't replied by Wednesday:

```
Hi Prof. [Last Name],

Following up on the email I sent Saturday about a summer RA slot. Forwarding
in case it got buried, and adding a quick concrete: I just put a demo gif of
Cloudify on the GitHub repo (github.com/anmolsahu2k/cloudify) that shows the
agent skill graph in action. Would love your reaction, or 15 minutes any time
this week.

Thanks,
Anmol
```

Move on if silent by Friday.
