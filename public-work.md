## 1. Writing

**"You're Shipping AI Output Without a Second Opinion. Here's How to Fix That."** (Feb 24, 2026)
Blog tab of https://anmolsahu2k.github.io/ (single-page site, no per-article permalink).
Two-model adversarial review: one agent produces, a second audits with a machine-parseable
verdict and a structured issue format, looping until approval or human escalation. Covers the
verdict protocol, the structured issue format, and why the auditor needs an explicit state
delta between rounds.

Companion code, public gist: https://gist.github.com/anmolsahu2k/715f4e705ed7fe77708c91ec90e5b219
A Claude Code slash command that grades Jupyter notebooks. Claude grades, Codex audits in
read-only mode, up to five rounds, final scores to CSV.
